### Context in BCRG & Protocol Modeling
This textbook serves as the foundational systems engineering methodology for the **Bonding Curve Research Group (BCRG)** and the **Avalanche Economic Systems Engineering** architecture.

Key concepts operationalized across BCRG models:
1. **Mission Elements Need Statement (MENS)**: Chapter 2 (Originating Requirements)
2. **Concept of Operations (ConOps)**: Chapter 3 (Operational Architecture & Scenarios)
3. **Economic Objectives Hierarchy (EOH)**: Chapters 4–5 (Value Trees & Stakeholder Objectives)
4. **Functional & Behavioral Architecture**: Chapters 6–8 (IDEF0, SysML Activity Diagrams, Stock & Flow Transitions)
5. **Physical & Mathematical Allocation**: Chapters 9–11 (Interface Control Documents [ICDs], Mathematical Verification Matrices)
